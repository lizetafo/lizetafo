# Hi, I'm Elizabeth Etafo 👋

**Ph.D. candidate in Pharmaceutical Sciences** at the University of Nebraska Medical Center (expected May 2027) and a licensed pharmacist.

I study how HIV persists in the brain and spinal cord, and how well antiretroviral therapy reaches it, using humanized mouse models. My work combines wet-lab biology with data analysis in **R** and **Python**: I build tools that turn sequencing and imaging data into reproducible results.

### 🚀 Projects
| Project | What it does |
|---|---|
| [**RNA-seq Shiny App**](https://github.com/lizetafo/rnaseq-shiny-app) | No-code R Shiny app for bulk RNA-seq: DESeq2 differential expression, gene annotation, volcano plots and heatmaps. Results validated against DESeq2. |

### 🔬 What I work on
- **Neuro-HIV and antiretroviral therapy in the CNS:** preclinical studies in a humanized microglial mouse model using ddPCR, multicolor flow cytometry, IHC/IF, multispectral imaging, and LC-MS/MS pharmacokinetics
- **Viral infectivity profiling:** generating HIV strain stocks and quantifying infectivity with TZM-bl luciferase reporter assays
- **RNA-seq analysis:** interactive **R Shiny** tools for differential expression and visualization, plus single-cell workflows with **Seurat** and **Scanpy**
- **Image analysis:** quantifying astrocyte and microglia morphology from multispectral immunofluorescence (R, EBImage, QuPath)
- **Nanoparticle drug delivery:** designing and characterizing polymeric nanoparticles (DLS, zeta potential, NMR)
