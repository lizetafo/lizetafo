# Hi, I'm Elizabeth Etafo 👋

**Ph.D. candidate in Pharmaceutical Sciences** at the University of Nebraska Medical Center and a **licensed pharmacist (Nigeria)**.

My expertise spans **neuroimmunology, preclinical pharmacology and drug delivery**, with hands-on experience in animal models, flow cytometry, molecular assays and fluorescence imaging. Alongside my lab work, I've trained in **R and Python** through coursework and self-study, and I enjoy building tools that make biological data analysis easier and more reproducible.
